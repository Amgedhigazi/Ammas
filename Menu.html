Our Menu

Every dish on our menu is a carefully crafted story of flavour — from sizzling grills and ocean-fresh seafood to handmade desserts that linger long after the last bite. Explore our world of taste, where every choice is a delicious discovery.

Appetizer

Finger Steaks — Beef strips encrusted with chef's special herb & pepper, served with green salad & mustard dip. Nachos topped with melted cheddar cheese, sour cream, jalapeno, tomato coriander salsa & guacamole.

Soup Menu

Fresh soup prepared daily — ask for today's selection: Cream of Mushroom, Vegetable Minestrone, Seafood Minestrone, Chicken Clear Soup, Tom Kha Gai, Cream of Chicken, French Onion, or Cumin Garlic Lentil soup.

Sea Food

Atlantic salmon marinated in dill herbs, served with seasonal grilled veg and your choice of sauce — creamy mushroom, garlic or pepper. Also: crumbed fried fish fillet with tartar sauce and side salad.

Steaks & Grill

T-Bone steak served with seasonal grilled or sautéed veg, French fries, jacket potato or mashed potato. Choice of creamy mushroom, garlic or pepper sauce.

Poultry

Crumbed fried fish fillet served with tartar sauce, French fries and a fresh side salad. Ask about our daily poultry specials for seasonal additions to the selection.

Chef Plate

Our special breaded grilled chicken, paired with our own mushroom onion sauce — the chef's personal recommendation for a rich and satisfying main course.

Beverages & Sweets

Warm apple pie with vanilla ice cream, Browniz Crème Caramel, and our full beverages selection. Homemade irresistible desserts to complement every meal. Just enjoy the taste.
