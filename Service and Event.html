Business Cocktail Receptions

It's about business. Follow the brief guidelines below and you will find yourself moving with confidence from one excellent business opportunity to the next at your next business event.

Hold your drink or food in your left hand. Your right hand must be clean, dry and available for shaking hands. There is no gracious way to juggle cocktail party food. The foods served at these events are called hors d'oeuvres or canapés. They are small snacks such as cheese, meat, or vegetables. Hors d'oeuvres may be eaten with fingers or a utensil. Follow these simple guidelines and you will always appear well-schooled and competent in business and social manners.

Social & Family Events

Birthday party bookings are first come, first served. All party options have a limit for participant capacity. All parties require the renter to supply their own food and decorations.

Party packages

For all party packages, payment must be completed a minimum of seven days prior to the party date. Room time includes set up time and clean up time by the renter. Parental supervision is required for all birthday parties and the exact number of participants must be identified when booking your reservation.

Arena party

Your arena party booking includes 50 minutes of ice time, followed by one hour in the community room for gifts and food. Maximum number of people: 25.

Outdoor and Pool Party

Booking includes one hour of pool time with a certified lifeguard, followed by one hour in the community room for gifts and food. Maximum number of people: 25.
